# Drawings

Dimensioned engineering drawings for fabrication, measurement, and reference.

**Intended contents**
- PDF drawings with critical dimensions and tolerances.
- Reference sketches for cabinet fit and mounting.

## Active OLED mount design direction

`Decca_OLED_Display_Mount_CAD_Review_revO.md` is the authoritative carrier
architecture brief. It includes the 2026-08-29 Rev P physical-retention
correction: flush-side insertion onto fixed rear PCB datums, positive sprung-post
handling retention, and no release until the corrected print passes physical
retention tests.

The four PNGs are generated by `snapshots()` in the Rev P generator, so they
always document the geometry that is actually in the `.f3d`.

Current mechanical revision: **P — OPEN, NOT RELEASED**. The first Rev P print
**failed its physical retention test**: the OLED fell forward out of the loose
carrier. The cause was architectural — rear insertion, shoulders restraining the
wrong direction, and forward retention left to assumed friction. Rev P.2 is the
corrected geometry and it passes every CAD and mesh check, but two things are
still open:

1. **one blocking measurement before any print** — the glass envelope at the two
   header-side mounting holes (build review §9); and
2. **the physical inversion and gentle-shake handling test** (build review §14),
   which is the only thing that can close the retention finding.

Module preparation is unchanged — front-side solder protrusion ≤ 1.00 mm.
