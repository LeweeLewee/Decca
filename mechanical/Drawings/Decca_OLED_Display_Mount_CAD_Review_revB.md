## 1. Specification readiness verdict

**Architecture: ready. Reference geometry: incomplete.**

The locked Decca interface (§2), the depth strategy (§7), the load path (§6) and the
appearance intent (§12) are internally consistent and were built exactly as written.

However §3 states the governing optical rule — *"OLED active-area centre must coincide
with the Decca opening centre. The PCB outline is not the optical datum"* — and then
supplies no dimension that locates the active area relative to the PCB or its mounting
holes. As written, §3 cannot be satisfied from its own numbers.

Verification attempted and failed to close the gap:
- The Pi Hut product page for SKU 105630 publishes **no mechanical drawing**.
- The SH1106 datasheet is the **controller IC** datasheet, not a module outline drawing.

Three numbers must therefore be measured off the physical module. They are exposed as
named parameters; correcting them is a parameter edit and a regenerate.

| Parameter | Used | Basis | How to measure |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `oled_active_off_y` | **4.00 mm** | **ASSUMED** — active-area centre above PCB centre | Callipers: PCB bottom edge → active-area bottom edge, then subtract. **Needs ±0.10 mm.** |
| `oled_hole_pitch_x` | **30.00 mm** | **ASSUMED** — horizontal mounting-hole pitch | Centre-to-centre across the two top holes. ±0.20 mm is fine. |
| `oled_glass_standoff` | **0.60 mm** | **ASSUMED** — PCB front face → glass rear face | Total module front stack minus glass thickness. ±0.10 mm. |

Secondary assumptions (lower consequence): glass panel 34.50 × 23.00 × 1.40 mm and
`oled_glass_off_y` 1.55 mm. These only size the carrier's clearance window, which
already carries 0.25 mm all round.

**Sensitivity — read this.** `oled_active_off_y` moves the display 1:1 against the
opening. The spec's own bezel window (15.10 mm) clears the active area (14.70 mm) by
only **0.20 mm per side vertically**. An error greater than 0.20 mm will clip the top or
bottom pixel rows behind the bezel. This is the single tightest tolerance in the design
and is inherent to the spec's numbers, not to this CAD.

Everything else in §1–§14 was buildable as specified.

## 2. What was built

```
Decca_Display_Mount
├── REF_Decca_Panel        3 mm Perspex, 35.50 × 15.80 opening, 2 × M2 clearance @ 48.00
├── REF_SH1106_1P3         PCB, glass, active area, header keep-out
├── Front_Bezel            40.00 × 20.30 × 1.90 mm    0.417 cm³
└── Rear_Display_Carrier   56.00 × 41.10 × 5.60 mm    3.324 cm³
```

**Datum / coordinate system.** Origin = Decca opening centre = OLED active-area centre.
Z = 0 at the **rear face of the Perspex**; +Z toward the viewer. Every depth derives from
the chain `carrier_front_t = screen_panel_gap + oled_glass_standoff + oled_glass_t`, so
the 0.30 mm optical gap of §7 is generated by hard carrier datums, exactly as required —
not by screw torque and not by compressing the OLED.

## 3. Decisions taken where the spec gave a range

| Item | Spec range | Chosen | Reason |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Carrier width | 54–56 mm | **56.00** | Gives 2.15 mm of material around the Ø3.20 insert bore in a Ø7.50 boss |
| Bezel outer | 39.5–40.5 × 19.8–20.8 | **40.00 × 20.30** | Mid-range |
| Bezel face thickness | 1.0–1.4 mm | **1.20** | Mid-range |
| Locating-lip depth | 0.6–0.8 mm | **0.70** | Mid-range |
| Structural wall | 1.6–2.0 mm | **1.80** | 4 perimeters at 0.45 mm |

**Three design calls worth flagging:**

1. **Bezel lip realised as four corner blocks, not a continuous rail.** A continuous lip
   inside the 35.50 × 15.80 opening would need top and bottom rails only **0.20 mm tall**
   (opening 15.80 less window 15.10, halved) — unprintable on FDM. Four corner blocks
   inside a 35.20 × 15.50 envelope locate the bezel in both X and Y with 0.15 mm
   clearance per side, satisfying §4's "clearance rather than interference".

2. **Retention split per §8:** two plain locating pins Ø2.70 in the **top** PCB holes
   (location only), two **split sprung snap pegs** in the **bottom** holes. The pegs
   flare to Ø3.30 immediately behind the PCB rear face — a 0.15 mm radial hook, which
   loads the PCB only and never the glass.

3. **The locating pins deliberately carry no root fillet.** §11 asks for R0.8–1.0 on
   stressed snap features; a fillet of that size at a Ø2.70 pin reaches Ø4.30 and cannot
   fit inside the Ø3.00 PCB hole. The snap pegs *do* get the R0.8 fillet, contained
   inside a **Ø4.60 × 1.00 mm counterbore relief** so it sits entirely forward of the PCB
   datum plane. The pins are location-only and lightly loaded, so a crisp root is correct.

## 4. Fault found and corrected during the build

An earlier iteration applied the §11 R0.8 fillets directly at the pin and peg roots on
the datum face. Boolean interference analysis caught it: the fillets projected **0.33 mm**
past the Ø3.00 hole wall, which would have held the PCB 0.33 mm off its datum and pushed
the OLED glass hard against the Perspex — a direct violation of §7 (nominal gap 0.30 mm
would have become −0.03 mm, i.e. contact).

Fixed by the counterbore relief described above. The final model re-validates clear.

A second issue: Fusion's fillet feature failed on the split peg roots
(`ASM_BL_CAP_COMPLEX`) and, on retry, applied asymmetrically to one peg only. The root
fillets are therefore built as **deterministic primitive geometry** (torus-derived true
radius), so both pegs are identical by construction.

## 6. Print guidance

- **Material:** PETG / PETG-HF (§11).
- **Carrier orientation:** Perspex seating face (Z = 0) **down on the bed**. This puts
  both critical datums — the Perspex seating plane and the PCB bearing plane — in flat
  layers, and prints the pins, pegs and bosses upward. **No supports required.**
- **Bezel orientation:** cosmetic front face **down on the bed** for the smoothest visible
  surface; the rear aperture chamfer then prints as a 45° overhang. Watch for elephant's
  foot on the R0.4 front edge — first-layer squish should be conservative.
- 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, ~40% infill. Walls dominate both parts.

**Hardware:**

| Item | Qty | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| M2 heat-set insert, Ø3.2 OD × 4.0 long | 2 | Installed from the front face of each boss |
| M2 × 8 mm bolt | 2 | Head + 3.00 Perspex + 0.40 recess + 4.00 insert |
| Cable tie, ≤3.5 mm wide | 1–2 | Through the two 4.0 × 1.8 mm flange slots |

The insert seats **0.40 mm below** the Perspex seating face, so brass never contacts the
original Perspex and bolt torque lands on printed plastic against the panel, per §6.

## 7. Open items for prototype validation

1. **Measure the three parameters in §1 and regenerate.** Do this before printing a part
   you intend to keep.
2. **Snap-peg force.** Barb hook is 0.15 mm radial; free length ~3.1 mm; peak bending
   strain ~2.1%, within PETG. Confirm on the first print — if retention feels light,
   raise `snap_barb_d` from 3.30.
3. **Top-edge lift.** Retention is at the bottom two holes; the top is held by pins in
   close-fitting holes. Adequate for a static display, but if the top edge lifts off its
   datum after assembly, convert the top pins to snap pegs — same parametric change.
4. **Bezel retention.** Per §4 the bezel is cosmetic and unattached; the corner blocks are
   clearance, not interference, so it *will* be loose until retained. Spec permits light
   removable adhesive after fit testing.
5. **Perspex fixing-hole diameter** modelled as Ø2.40 (standard M2 clearance). §2 says
   only "M2 clearance" — confirm against the original panel.
